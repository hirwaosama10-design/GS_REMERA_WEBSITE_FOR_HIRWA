Our History

G.S Remera TSS was established in 2015 with a vision to provide quality education to students in Rulindo District and beyond. Since our founding, we have grown from a small institution to a comprehensive educational center serving over 500 students across multiple programs.

Over the years, we have expanded our facilities, enhanced our curriculum, and built a team of dedicated educators committed to student success. Our graduates have gone on to excel in universities and careers across Rwanda and internationally.

Our Mission

To provide quality education for sustainable development, empowering students with knowledge, skills, and values for lifelong success and meaningful contribution to society.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in education, producing competent, innovative, and responsible graduates who drive national development.
